# DC Risk Register

Lifecycle risk register and leadership dashboard for data center development projects.

**Live:** https://dc-risk.vercel.app

## What it does

Describe a project in about twenty fields (state, capacity, phase, power status, cooling, contract structure, financing, insurance and compliance programs). The register evaluates 72 curated risks against that profile, scores each on a 5 × 5 likelihood and impact matrix, and produces a one-page memo for leadership.

The goal is a register that stays current across site selection, permitting, procurement, construction, commissioning, and operations, rather than a spreadsheet refreshed quarterly.

## How scoring works

Every score is rule-based and reproducible.

- Each risk has a base likelihood and base impact (1 to 5).
- Trigger rules adjust those values from the project profile. Example: long-lead electrical equipment gains likelihood when no POs are placed and when fewer than 24 months remain to COD, and loses likelihood once orders are confirmed.
- Score = likelihood × impact (1 to 25). Bands: Low 1–4, Medium 5–9, High 10–15, Critical 16–25.
- Risks are classified against the current phase: active, upcoming, or retired. Only active risks feed the summary, matrix, and memo.

Structure follows ISO 31000: identification (which risks apply), analysis (trigger rules), evaluation (bands and ranking), treatment (mitigations and KRIs on every row). The heat matrix follows COSO-style presentation.

## Unknown inputs

Any input can be set to Unknown. Triggers that depend on an unknown field do not fire and the risk is flagged as unverified. The register shows which inputs are missing and which scores depend on them; the memo lists what must be confirmed. This keeps a blank from being read as a favorable answer.

## Leadership memo

Generated by an AI model from the scored register. The model does not score, rank, or add risks. It interprets the deterministic output, connects compounding risks, and recommends where attention goes in the next 90 days.

## Data and limitations

- Risk library, base scores, and trigger rules: curated by the author from public industry sources and professional judgment.
- State ratings (hazards, grid constraint, labor, policy): ordinal estimates (0 to 3) informed by FEMA National Risk Index, NOAA climate summaries, and ISO/RTO reporting. Not measured values. Not drawn from any company's proprietary data.
- Prototype only. For production: calibrate base scores against portfolio incident history, replace state ratings with county-level sourced data, persist and version the register.
